## I. Introduction

> *"What if we could understand this better through data?"*

That's usually where it starts.

I enjoy the process of turning an interesting question into something I can investigate: reading papers, exploring datasets, testing hypotheses, building models, and occasionally discovering that my initial assumptions were completely wrong.

My interests aren't limited to one domain. I'm particularly drawn to problems where data, computation, and real-world needs intersect. But if you want to know though, I'm particularly drawn to mental health and human psychology as domains where data science and artificial intelligence can contribute to a deeper understanding of human behavior, emotional well-being, and psychological processes.

## III. Selected Investigations

*A collection of research questions, datasets, experiments, and ideas that somehow became projects.*

### [01] PURE — Pulse Understanding & Reliability Engine

**Domain:** Biomedical AI · Physiological Signal Processing · IoT

**Research Question:** How can AI help determine whether a PPG heart-rate measurement is reliable before it is presented to healthcare personnel?

**Approach:** Developed a multimodal signal-quality assessment prototype combining PPG waveform analysis, motion information, and machine learning to evaluate measurement reliability and provide corrective guidance.

**Recognition:** Top 46 out of 170 ideas · International hackathon with participation from 7+ countries.

**Repository:** [itsceyuu/pure-pulsesure](https://github.com/itsceyuu/pure-pulsesure)

---

### [02] The Usage LLM in Client Psychological Graph (CPG) Making

**Domain:** Mental Health · Natural Language Processing · Computational Psychology

**Research Question:** How can psychological processes and behavioral patterns be represented computationally to support more coherent and psychologically grounded AI systems?

**Approach:** Exploring Indonesian-language synthetic counseling data, psychological process annotations, and structured representations of client psychological states and their relationships across multi-turn interactions.

**Domain:** Computer Vision · Optical Character Recognition · Applied AI

**Role:** Project Manager & AI Research

**Research Question:** How can optical character recognition and document-processing techniques improve the extraction and organization of information from document images?

**Approach:** Contributed to the research direction and technical planning of an OCR-based system, including investigating suitable recognition approaches, coordinating project development, and aligning the AI component with project requirements.

### [05] Retail Sales ELT — From Raw Transactions to Business Insights

**Domain:** Data Engineering · Business Intelligence · Predictive Analytics

**Research Question:** How can raw retail transaction data be transformed into reliable analytical datasets that support business performance monitoring and sales forecasting?

**Approach:** Developed an end-to-end ELT pipeline using a Bronze–Silver–Gold architecture, integrating ClickHouse, dbt, and Python for data transformation, Metabase for business intelligence dashboards, and machine learning for sales forecasting.

**Technologies:** Python · SQL · ClickHouse · dbt · Metabase · Scikit-learn · XGBoost · Docker

**Repository:** [itsceyuu/Retail-Sales-ELT](https://github.com/itsceyuu/Retail-Sales-ELT)
